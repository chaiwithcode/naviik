Naviik support

Control a compatible Google TV or Android TV from your iPhone or iPad.

Connect your TV

  1. Connect your iPhone or iPad and TV to the same local network.
  2. Keep the TV awake and allow Naviik to access your local network.
  3. Choose Pair TV, select your TV and enter the PIN shown on the TV.

If your TV does not appear

Check that both devices are on the same network. Guest Wi-Fi, VPNs and router isolation can prevent discovery. Enable Naviik under Settings → Privacy & Security → Local Network. Restart the app and TV if necessary.

Compatibility

Naviik supports compatible Google TV and Android TV devices with the network remote service enabled. Voice, keyboard, app launching and power commands depend on your TV, software and active app. A sleeping TV may need its physical remote to wake up. This version does not connect to Samsung Tizen, LG webOS, Roku or Apple TV hardware.

Voice and keyboard

Allow microphone access to use voice input. Your TV needs a compatible assistant. Keyboard input is available only when the TV and active field support it.

Contact us

Email support.chaiwithcode@gmail.com with your TV model, TV software version, iPhone or iPad model, iOS version, Naviik version and a description of the problem. Please do not include pairing PINs or passwords.
