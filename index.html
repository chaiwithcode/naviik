Get started with Naviik

Find Naviik on the App Store, then pair your TV in three steps.

1

Connect to Wi-Fi

Ensure your iPhone or iPad is connected to the exact same Wi-Fi network as your Google TV or Android TV.

2

Select your TV

Open Naviik. Allow local network access, scan for compatible TVs, and select yours.

3

Enter the pairing code

A secure pairing code will appear on your TV screen. Type it into Naviik, and you are ready to go.

Frequently Asked Questions

Which TVs are supported?

Naviik supports devices running Google TV and Android TV. Compatibility depends on your TV’s Android TV remote service and network settings. Your phone and TV must be on the same local network.

Note: Naviik does not support Samsung (Tizen), LG (webOS), Roku, or Apple TV devices.

Where can I get Naviik?

Visit Naviik’s App Store page on your iPhone or iPad.

Do I need an iPhone with an IR blaster?

No. Naviik communicates with your TV securely over your local Wi-Fi network. No line-of-sight or infrared hardware is required.

Can I stream the Apple TV app to my Google TV?

Naviik can launch the Apple TV streaming app if it is installed on your Google TV device, but Naviik is a remote control, not a media casting tool.
